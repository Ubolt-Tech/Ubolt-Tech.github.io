About Ubolt

One connected platform, order to ledger

Ubolt runs the full journey of a load — from booking and trip planning to delivery, billing, and accounting — in one connected platform. Operations, compliance, and finance work off the same data, so a consignment moves through to a GST invoice and into your books without re-keying.

Everything runs on enterprise cloud, with security, monitoring, and scale built in. GST e-invoicing, e-way bills, and TDS are handled inside the platform, so Indian compliance is part of the product — not an add-on you stitch together.

Finance Copilot

Your Books, Answered.

Finance Copilot sits inside your accounting and answers in plain language. No report-hunting, no spreadsheets, no waiting on the finance team. Ask a question, get the number — backed by your real ledger.

See Finance Copilot in action

What it does

  • Plain-language answers — Ask "What's my profit this quarter?" or "Show outstanding by customer" and get a clear, sourced reply.
  • Live statements on demand — Balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow, and trial balance, generated from current data.
  • Built-in financial analysis — Key ratios and period-over-period trends, explained in business terms.
  • Charts and summaries — Turns answers into charts and short write-ups you can act on or share.
  • Compliance-aware — Surfaces TDS and transaction anomalies alongside the answer, so issues don't slip past.
  • Conversational and saved — Threaded chats keep context, so follow-up questions just work.

Who it helps

  • Owners and management — Instant visibility into cash, profit, and outstanding — without waiting for a report cycle.
  • Finance and accounts teams — Faster month-end close and fewer manual report pulls.
  • Auditors and compliance — Anomaly flags and TDS checks with plain explanations and an audit-ready trail.
  • Operations and branch heads — Ask about trip costs, settlements, and dues directly, with no accounting screens to learn.
FleetSense

Know Where Every Load Is.

FleetSense keeps your whole fleet on one live map and keeps trip status current on its own — so your team spends less time chasing updates and more time moving freight.

  • Real-time GPS tracking — See every vehicle's live location across the fleet on a single map.
  • Automatic stop & waiting detection — Geofencing detects arrivals and waiting time without anyone pressing a button.
  • Live trip status — Trip stages update from the vehicle's location, so dashboards stay current automatically.
  • Full route history — Replay where a vehicle has been across the trip for verification and disputes.
  • Works with your existing feeds — Connects to OEM vehicle feeds and GPS devices, so you keep the hardware you already run.
